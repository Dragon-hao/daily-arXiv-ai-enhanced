### [1] [DeltaWAM: Delta World Action Models for Bimanual Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28811)
*Han Yan,Zishang Xiang,Haokai Jiang,Zeyu Zhang,Qilin Wang,Weiyu Guo,Yandong Guo,Boxin Shi,Hao Tang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: DeltaWAM 提出一种"世界-动作模型"新范式：不再预测稠密未来帧，而是联合预测"视觉增量（visual delta）"与动作，并配合流式增量记忆（SDM）降低推理开销，在机器人操控任务上同时提升成功率与效率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有世界-动作模型（WAMs）借助大规模预训练视频生成器，将视觉与运动先验迁移到机器人控制，通过联合建模视觉动态与动作来提升策略学习。但存在两个核心问题：(1) 训练时需预测稠密未来帧，而相邻帧间大量内容几乎不变，造成重复计算浪费；同时把动作条件动态与无关的外观变化（nuisance appearance variations）耦合在一起，干扰动作学习。(2) 推理时每一步都要用重量级视频专家处理完整观测，导致少步（few-step）动作生成被严重瓶颈化。论文旨在让 WAM 聚焦于真正变化的部分，并降低推理时对重型视频专家的依赖，从而兼顾精度与效率。

Method: 核心思路是用"稠密锚点 + 稀疏增量 + 动作"三路流替代稠密帧预测：稠密锚点（dense-anchor）流保留基础视觉上下文，稀疏增量（sparse-delta）流只建模发生变化的视觉增量，动作流则负责动作生成。基于这三路流，论文设计了三种架构，区别在于表示形式与模块间计算共享的方式。在此基础上提出流式增量记忆（Streaming Delta Memory, SDM）：用紧凑的已观测增量持续更新缓存的锚点上下文（anchor context），避免每一步都重新调用重型视频专家进行完整处理，从而减少推理计算。

Result: 在 RoboTwin 基准上，DeltaWAM + SDM 相较 Fast-WAM 平均成功率从 81.3% 提升至 85.4%（干净设定），在视觉随机化设定下从 75.8% 提升至 83.9%。效率方面，三种架构降低训练 FLOPs 17.78%–23.77%；SDM 使单步推理延迟和 FLOPs 分别降低 36.57% 和 31.55%。真实世界实验显示，在所评估的策略中取得最高总体成功率和归一化进度（normalized progress）。

Conclusion: 论文的主要贡献在于：提出以视觉增量与动作联合预测替代稠密帧重建的 DeltaWAM 框架，并给出三种计算共享架构；提出 SDM 机制以增量更新锚点上下文，显著降低推理延迟与计算量。在仿真与真实机器人操控上同时取得更高成功率与更高效率，且受益于显式解耦外观变化，在视觉随机化下鲁棒性提升尤为明显。潜在局限在于：摘要未说明三种架构的选型准则、增量稀疏度的判定方式，以及 SDM 长时间累积更新可能带来的缓存误差问题；实验主要围绕操控任务，未验证在更复杂的动态场景或事件相机等极端感知模态下的适用性。

Abstract: World-action models (WAMs) transfer visual and motion priors from pretrained video generators to robot control by jointly modeling visual dynamics and actions. Existing WAMs, however, predict dense future frames during training, repeatedly modeling largely unchanged content and coupling action-conditioned dynamics to nuisance appearance variations. At inference, processing each complete observation with the heavy video expert bottlenecks few-step action generation. Accordingly, we propose DeltaWAM, which jointly predicts visual deltas and actions using dense-anchor, sparse-delta, and action streams, with three architectures that differ in representation and computation sharing. We further develop Streaming Delta Memory (SDM), which updates cached anchor context with compact observed deltas, reducing heavy video-expert processing. On RoboTwin, DeltaWAM with SDM improves average success over Fast-WAM from 81.3% to 85.4% in the clean setting and from 75.8% to 83.9% under visual randomization. The three architectures reduce training FLOPs by 17.78-23.77%, while SDM reduces one-step inference latency and FLOPs by 36.57% and 31.55%, respectively; real-world evaluations further show the highest overall success rate and normalized progress among the evaluated policies. Code: https://github.com/AIGeeksGroup/DeltaWAM. Website: https://aigeeksgroup.github.io/DeltaWAM.

Conclusion: 主要贡献在于把耦合的多模态偏好学习分解为单模态子问题，从而实现精确奖励归因并改善跨模态同步，同时通过冻结塔优化显著降低训练成本；配套的 5DAV 数据集为难度可控训练提供了系统化支持。局限方面：论文摘要未给出定量指标与具体同步度量细节，同步评估仍依赖成对样本设计，且冻结塔策略可能在两模态动态耦合较强时引入优化偏差，泛化性有待更广泛验证。

Existing models still suffer from limited per-modality fidelity, insufficient text-modality alignment and weak cross-modal synchronization. Moreover, synchronization evaluation difficulty depends on paired samples, preventing fair reward comparisons. AV-GRPO includes three key modules: (1) modality-anchored rollouts to disentangle learning signals and stabilize difficulty; (2) trajectory-locked frozen-tower optimization to reduce cost and reassign credit; (3) adaptive objectives and perturbation strengths tailored to modality-specific dynamics. This converts coupled multimodal preference learning into unimodal subproblems for precise reward attribution and better synchronization. Our 5DAV dataset decouples samples across five dimensions for systematic training. Experiments on JavisBench and VABench demonstrate AV-GRPO outperforms LTX-2.3 in generation quality, semantic alignment and cross-modal synchronization under LoRA and full fine-tuning. Ablations confirm our designs. Code and data: https://github.com/zhiyuxu03/AV-GRPO
