### [1] [AV-GRPO: Modality-Anchored Decoupling Diffusion Reinforcement Learning for Joint Audio-Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29816)
*Zhiyu Xu,Weilong Yan,Yufei Shi,Shiyang Li,Yihao Liu,Kin-Man Lam,Yuewen Cao*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出 AV-GRPO，一个面向联合音视频生成的"模态锚定"在线扩散强化学习（diffusion RL）后训练框架，并配套发布解耦、难度可控的训练数据集 5DAV。核心思想是将耦合的多模态偏好学习分解为单模态子问题，从而提升奖励归因精度与跨模态同步性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有联合音视频生成模型存在三大缺陷：单模态保真度有限、文本-模态对齐不足、跨模态同步性弱。强化学习后训练（RL post-training）被认为是有效补救手段，但直接应用于音视频联合生成面临三重挑战：(1) 异构多模态奖励相互纠缠，导致学习信号混杂、信用分配困难；(2) 两个模态塔（视频塔与音频塔）动力学差异大，联合优化的计算成本高昂；(3) 同步性评估依赖成对样本，难以进行公平的奖励比较。

Method: 提出 AV-GRPO，一种模态锚定的在线扩散 RL 框架，包含三个关键模块：(1) 模态锚定 rollout（modality-anchored rollouts）：解耦学习信号并稳定难度分布；(2) 轨迹锁定冻结塔优化（trajectory-locked frozen-tower optimization）：降低计算成本并重新分配信用；(3) 自适应目标与扰动强度（adaptive objectives and perturbation strengths）：针对各模态专属动力学进行定制。该设计将耦合的多模态偏好学习转化为若干单模态子问题，实现更精确的奖励归因与更好的同步性。同时构建 5DAV 数据集，沿五个维度解耦样本以支持系统性训练。

Result: 在 JavisBench 与 VABench 两个基准上进行实验，结果表明在 LoRA 微调与全量微调两种设置下，AV-GRPO 在生成质量、语义对齐和跨模态同步性三方面均优于 LTX-2.3 基线。消融实验验证了各模块设计的有效性。代码与数据已开源（github.com/zhiyuxu03/AV-GRPO）。

Conclusion: 主要贡献在于：将扩散 RL 后训练从单模态扩展到联合音视频生成，通过模态锚定 rollout 与冻结塔优化解决异构奖励纠缠与计算成本问题，并配套难度可控的解耦数据集 5DAV。意义在于为多模态生成模型的 RL 对齐提供了可分解、可归因的训练范式。潜在局限：抽象中未明确报告具体量化指标与同步度量方式；模态锚定 rollout 可能对模态间强耦合场景的建模能力带来一定约束；数据集维度选择的通用性有待验证。

Existing models still suffer from limited per-modality fidelity, insufficient text-modality alignment and weak cross-modal synchronization. While reinforcement-learning post-training offers a promising remedy, directly adapting it to joint audio-video generation is challenging. Heterogeneous multimodal rewards entangle learning signals and complicate credit assignment. Joint optimization of two modality towers is computationally expensive given their divergent dynamics. Moreover, synchronization evaluation difficulty depends on paired samples, preventing fair reward comparisons. We propose AV-GRPO, a modality-anchored online diffusion RL framework, and 5DAV, a decoupled, difficulty-controllable training dataset. AV-GRPO includes three key modules: (1) modality-anchored rollouts to disentangle learning signals and stabilize difficulty; (2) trajectory-locked frozen-tower optimization to reduce cost and reassign credit; (3) adaptive objectives and perturbation strengths tailored to modality-specific dynamics. This converts coupled multimodal preference learning into unimodal subproblems for precise reward attribution and better synchronization. Our 5DAV dataset decouples samples across five dimensions for systematic training. Experiments on JavisBench and VABench demonstrate AV-GRPO outperforms LTX-2.3 in generation quality, semantic alignment and cross-modal synchronization under LoRA and full fine-tuning. Ablations confirm our designs. Code and data: https://github.com/zhiyuxu03/AV-GRPO

### [2] [DeltaWAM: Delta World Action Models for Bimanual Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28811)
*Han Yan,Zishang Xiang,Haokai Jiang,Zeyu Zhang,Qilin Wang,Weiyu Guo,Yandong Guo,Boxin Shi,Hao Tang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: DeltaWAM 提出用"密集锚点+稀疏增量+动作"三流联合建模的 world-action model，通过学习视觉增量（delta）而非稠密未来帧来预测动作，并设计 Streaming Delta Memory（SDM）复用缓存的锚点上下文以减少重型视频专家计算。在 RoboTwin 仿真上成功率从 Fast-WAM 的 81.3% 提升到 85.4%（clean）和 75.8%→83.9%（视觉随机化），训练 FLOPs 降低 17.78–23.77%，SDM 使单步推理延迟和 FLOPs 分别降低 36.57% 和 31.55%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: World-action models（WAMs）通过联合建模视觉动态与动作，把预训练视频生成器中的视觉与运动先验迁移到机器人控制中。但现有 WAMs 存在两个核心问题：（1）训练阶段预测稠密未来帧，反复建模大部分未变化的内容，并且把动作条件动态与"冗余的外观变化（nuisance appearance variations）"耦合在一起，降低了学习效率与表征的判别性；（2）推理阶段对每个完整观测都用重型视频专家（video expert）进行处理，成为少步动作生成的瓶颈，导致推理延迟高、FLOPs 大，难以满足实时机器人控制的需求。

Method: 核心思路是用"增量（delta）"代替稠密未来帧预测。模型包含三条流：dense-anchor 流（保持对当前观测/锚点上下文的稠密建模）、sparse-delta 流（仅建模视觉变化量）、action 流（生成动作），并给出三种在表征与计算共享方式上不同的架构变体。此外提出 Streaming Delta Memory（SDM）：用观测到的紧凑 delta 增量式更新缓存的 anchor 上下文，从而避免对每个完整观测都重新运行重型视频专家。该设计将动作条件动态与外观噪声解耦，并显著减少视频专家的重复计算。

Result: 在 RoboTwin 基准上，DeltaWAM+SDM 相较 Fast-WAM 将平均成功率从 81.3% 提升到 85.4%（clean 设置），在视觉随机化（visual randomization）下从 75.8% 提升到 83.9%，显示更强的外观扰动鲁棒性。三种架构变体使训练 FLOPs 降低 17.78–23.77%；SDM 使单步推理延迟和 FLOPs 分别降低 36.57% 与 31.55%。真实世界（real-world）评测中，在所对比的策略里取得最高的总体成功率和归一化进展（normalized progress）。

Conclusion: DeltaWAM 通过"稀疏增量+密集锚点+动作"的联合建模范式与 SDM 流式记忆机制，缓解了 WAMs 中稠密帧预测的冗余和视频专家推理瓶颈，在成功率、鲁棒性与计算效率（训练/推理 FLOPs、延迟）上同时取得改进，并验证了真实世界泛化能力。可能的局限在于：论文摘要主要在 RoboTwin 仿真基准上量化结果，真实世界结论以总体成功率和归一化进展描述而缺少细化数据；增量建模范式对剧烈场景变化或长时域任务的有效性，以及三种架构变体在更广泛机器人平台上的可扩展性，仍有待更充分的验证。

Abstract: World-action models (WAMs) transfer visual and motion priors from pretrained video generators to robot control by jointly modeling visual dynamics and actions. Existing WAMs, however, predict dense future frames during training, repeatedly modeling largely unchanged content and coupling action-conditioned dynamics to nuisance appearance variations. At inference, processing each complete observation with the heavy video expert bottlenecks few-step action generation. Accordingly, we propose DeltaWAM, which jointly predicts visual deltas and actions using dense-anchor, sparse-delta, and action streams, with three architectures that differ in representation and computation sharing. We further develop Streaming Delta Memory (SDM), which updates cached anchor context with compact observed deltas, reducing heavy video-expert processing. On RoboTwin, DeltaWAM with SDM improves average success over Fast-WAM from 81.3% to 85.4% in the clean setting and from 75.8% to 83.9% under visual randomization. The three architectures reduce training FLOPs by 17.78-23.77%, while SDM reduces one-step inference latency and FLOPs by 36.57% and 31.55%, respectively; real-world evaluations further show the highest overall success rate and normalized progress among the evaluated policies. Code: https://github.com/AIGeeksGroup/DeltaWAM. Website: https://aigeeksgroup.github.io/DeltaWAM.

Ultimately, WanPE leads all evaluated commercial offerings at 5-15 seconds and remains competitive with Seedance 2.5 at 30 seconds.

  <summary>Details</summary>

The workspace has three properties. Contact views, selected automatically from the scene geometry, present the scene around the current interaction. Action rehearsal turns each action into an editable proposal that the agent, alone or through an Imagination Agent, previews and revises against planning feedback before execution. In-view correction closes the loop between observation, rehearsal, and low-level execution, letting the agent remove residual offsets in the view where it observes them. Abstract: Coding agents have demonstrated enormous success in solving complex programming problems. To leverage their potential for robot systems, this work introduces Robot Agentic Programming from Demonstrations (RAPID), which automatically generates, verifies, and refines robot programs, given a single visual human demonstration. The iterative agentic loop of code refinement requires several key ingredients: (i) a testable task specification, (ii) action primitives for robot execution, and (iii) an interactive environment for program execution and verification.
